02 · COMFORT MATTERS

How to choose the three mattresses

For most everyday adult sleepers, a supportive medium-to-medium-firm feel is a sensible place to start. Firmness preferences, body weight, sleep position and temperature can change the best choice.

✓ Confirm internal mattress size

King 78 × 72 in; queen 78 × 60 in in this plan. Ask the bed seller for the actual mattress recess measurement before ordering.

✓ Try each mattress in person

Lie down for 10–15 minutes in your usual sleeping position. Check shoulder/hip pressure, ease of turning and edge support.

✓ Don't trust “orthopedic” as a guarantee

It is a product label, not proof a mattress treats back pain. Comfort and support should be checked with the people who will use it.

✓ Verify trial and warranty

Compare online trial terms, delivery areas, return logistics, sagging exclusions and whether a local showroom purchase has the same policy.

04 · PURCHASE SMARTER

Checklist before we pay

  • Measure each room, doorway and staircase
  • Confirm king 78 × 72 and queen 78 × 60 mattress fit
  • Specify solid wood / plywood / MDF / particle board in writing
  • Ask for frame timber, board thickness and centre support
  • Test sofa cushions and dining chair stability
  • Inspect cot storage hinges, supports and accessibility
  • Try mattresses for 10–15 minutes, both sleepers if shared
  • Get an itemised quote including GST, delivery and assembly
  • Confirm defects, sagging, trial and warranty exclusions
  • Compare at least two local package quotes against this report
